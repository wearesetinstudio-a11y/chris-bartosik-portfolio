## Starting from product energy

Mexi Munchies needed a logo that could carry the personality of the product line without losing clarity across packaging, social content, and retail environments.

## Designing for culture and commerce

We focused on a mark that feels joyful and ownable while staying legible at shelf scale and in small digital formats.

## The result

The identity now reads instantly in competitive retail environments while leaving room for the brand to scale across new product lines.
