overviewText: |-
  Following a successful brand identity redesign, See More Logistics invited me to build their new digital platform. I designed a clean, lightweight UI/UX system in Figma and developed the live site directly in Webflow, integrating custom interactive background animations built with Rive.

  The website reflects a modern corporate aesthetic while maintaining an approachable, local-first user journey optimized for Panamanian business owners and logistics partners.
challengesAfterGroup: 2
strategyAfterGroup: 5
challengesText: |-
  Logistics websites often suffer from cluttered interfaces and generic global claims that alienate local business clients. The main challenge was to balance a minimal, white-space visual aesthetic with local commercial habits.

  In Panama, business communication relies heavily on instant messaging. The primary UX friction was converting visitors without forcing them through lengthy contact forms, requiring a seamless WhatsApp integration embedded directly into the primary navigation without breaking corporate polish.
strategyTitle: 'A local-first conversion strategy with Rive motion'
strategyText: |-
  To solve the conversion challenge, I prioritized a high-contrast, dedicated WhatsApp action button within the main navigation, giving instant access to sales reps. I engineered a modular component grid in Figma that allowed rapid deployment into Webflow.

  To give the clean white UI a subtle layer of dynamic polish, I designed and integrated interactive Rive background state-machines. These micro-interactions evoke motion and structural precision—core pillars of logistics—while maintaining ultra-fast page load times and responsive performance across all devices.
