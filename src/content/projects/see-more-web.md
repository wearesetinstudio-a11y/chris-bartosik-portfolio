overviewText: |-
  Following the creation of their brand identity, See More Logistics partnered with us to build their first web presence. We started with a discovery workshop and business interviews to map out their commercial goals, core services encompassing heavy FTL freight, last-mile urban distribution, and customs brokerage, alongside their lead generation requirements. These insights defined the scope for a focused Minimum Viable Product. This lean approach allowed a fast market launch while creating a scalable technical foundation for future client-facing logistics tools.
sections:
  - label: Strategy
    title: Positioning Against Legacy Operators
    text: |-
      Competitive research across the Panamanian logistics sector revealed a clear market gap, as large multinational operators often trap clients in rigid ticketing portals and slow bureaucracy, while smaller local carriers lack basic online credibility. We positioned See More Logistics as a responsive, high-accountability partner. The messaging highlights direct human coordination, real-time cargo visibility, and stress-free customs handling, combining modern product aesthetics with proven operational metrics including over one thousand monthly deliveries and a high on-time delivery rate.
    afterGroup: 1
  - label: UX/UI Design
    title: Information Architecture and Conversion Flows
    text: |-
      The interface structure is tailored specifically to B2B decision-makers in Panama, where the core objective was to present fleet capacity, national coverage, and institutional trust signals immediately upon arrival. The hero section anchors the brand in its local territory through a simplified architectural silhouette of Panama merging with a moving delivery truck, introduced by an animated logo preloader. Persistent navigation keeps key sections accessible at all times. To reduce friction in the sales funnel, we replaced generic contact forms with a segmented B2B quote flow allowing users to specify FTL freight, local distribution, or customs brokerage, paired with direct WhatsApp dispatch triggers that match local business communication habits. Social proof and government partnership credentials sit directly above the conversion points to reinforce security.
    afterGroup: 2
  - label: Engineering
    title: Frontend Architecture and Interactive Motion
    text: |-
      Building on an Astro architecture paired with Cursor workflows allowed us to complete full frontend development in two business days. Instead of spending cycles on boilerplate setup, we focused engineering time on performance optimization and custom motion mechanics. A vector-based Rive animation powers the hero landscape with a moving vehicle that scales across viewports without frame drops or heavy asset loads. Lottie drives the entry preloader and service card animations exported directly from Figma vectors. Furthermore, section dividers act as a continuous road network where geometric trucks travel across the screen, programmed with contextual state logic so that when a vehicle enters a dark navy section, its headlights turn on automatically.
    afterGroup: 3
  - label: Deployment
    title: Design System and Commercial Readiness
    text: |-
      The build produced a modular Figma Design System and reusable code components, unifying typography, spacing grids, and UI states so future pages or tracking portals can be shipped rapidly. The live MVP achieved all targets defined during discovery by replacing outdated industry tropes with a structured B2B interface, connecting operational credibility with direct WhatsApp and quote-request conversion paths, and integrating Google Analytics to monitor traffic sources, scroll depth, and lead conversion events.
