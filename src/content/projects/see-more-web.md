overviewText: |-
  Following our successful rebranding collaboration, See More Logistics expanded our partnership to create their first-ever corporate website. With no prior analytics or web data to rely on, I designed and developed a streamlined Webflow site tailored for market validation. The layout focuses on immediate business context, building trust with B2B decision-makers, and offering frictionless contact options like WhatsApp.
sections:
  - label: Retention & First Impression
    afterGroup: 2
    title: Establishing Instant Brand Identity Within the Critical 3-Second Window
    text: |-
      Launching without historical traffic data carried a high risk of bounce rates if visitors couldn't immediately identify the core service. To eliminate ambiguity, I replaced standard static hero visuals with a custom, looping vector animation featuring the signature blue delivery vehicle against the Panama City skyline. This visual hook immediately anchors user focus, extends initial session duration, and unambiguously communicates a regional logistics presence from the very first frame.
  - label: B2B Credibility
    afterGroup: 3
    title: Heavyweight Social Proof Engineered for Logistics Decision-Makers
    text: |-
      Target users are logistics operations managers whose primary decision driver is operational reliability. Operating under the hypothesis that written promises alone are insufficient, I followed conversion-focused landing page best practices by placing a high-visibility partner logo marquee directly below the Hero fold, followed by concrete performance metrics (5 years in operation, 100% national coverage, and 99.8% on-time delivery). This establishes corporate authority and trust before introducing specific services.
  - label: Conversion Strategy
    afterGroup: 5
    title: Defining the Primary Business Goal and Optimizing the Contact Path
    text: |-
      Working closely with the business, we defined the primary objective of the site: driving direct inquiries and initiating instant lead conversations. However, traditional multi-field forms create unnecessary friction in a regional market where WhatsApp is the dominant B2B communication tool. To minimize the path to conversion, I designed a mobile-first navigation bar featuring a fixed, high-contrast WhatsApp CTA button. This allows decision-makers to bypass formal email exchanges and open a direct dialogue with the sales team in a single tap.
  - label: Summary
    title: A Strategic Web Asset Engineered for Market Validation and Lead Capture
    text: |-
      The inaugural website for See More Logistics connects their updated visual identity directly to local B2B market dynamics. Rather than launching an overly complex portal, I developed a streamlined Webflow platform that immediately anchors brand context, establishes operational credibility through hard metrics, and eliminates conversion friction. It provides the company with a scalable, data-ready framework designed to capture corporate accounts across Panama.
