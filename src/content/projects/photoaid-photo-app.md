overviewText: >-
  In parallel with the development of our printing hardware terminals, we designed from scratch a dedicated mobile application to serve as the digital extension of the self-service kiosk ecosystem. While the core PhotoAiD app focused strictly on regulatory biometric documentation, this new interface was engineered for personalized souvenirs, greeting cards, and express retail print products. The interface layout was structured to support traditional photography formats, custom memory prints, and layouts generated by artificial intelligence.
  
  Our primary business objective focused on driving physical foot traffic to our retail kiosks and creating a seamless connection between mobile preparation and immediate on-site order collection. The application served a dual role, operating as a standalone e-commerce photo editor and as an agile promotional platform deployed to analyze user price sensitivity through dynamic discount systems.The project demanded a methodical evolution of our established visual guidelines. We built the interface upon our verified design pillars and corporate yellow palette, but moved away from rigid regulatory aesthetics toward a welcoming typography and friendlier interface components. We delivered a cohesive end-to-end framework managing a clear product catalog, a bulk print editor, an interactive checkout featuring Rive animations, and real-time pickup code delivery.

    text: >-
      A primary goal for this digital release involved triggering immediate conversion traffic to physical print terminals and evaluating consumer sensitivity toward structural price discounts. We linked mobile conversion pipelines to in store voucher validation events, tracking user cross-channel conversion with absolute accuracy.
