overviewTitle: Fearless snack culture and sweet energy
overviewText: |-
  Panamanians love snacks, and many of the best imports come straight from the United States. From crunchy cookies and chips to everyday sweets and sugary cereals, this category thrives on genuine joy. Snacks Lovers has built its position as a leading supplier of American snacks in Panama by tapping directly into this demand. My objective was to translate that raw, playful energy into a structured, scalable brand system. Moving away from scattered visuals, I crafted a cohesive identity that connects with customers on an emotional level while building lasting market equity.
sections:
  - label: Symbol
    title: A conversation between taste and emotion
    text: |-
      Identity lives in the details, so I built the core logotype around a playful dialogue between the words Snacks and Lovers. The defining move transforms the letter 'o' into a lollipop—a subtle, instantly recognizable nod to the products at the heart of the business. I wanted the mark to trigger a direct, intuitive craving while maintaining clean execution. By implementing a robust black and white outline, I ensured the logo punches through any background without losing integrity. I also established a precise clear space around the mark to give it room to breathe and maintain visual authority across touchpoints.
    afterGroup: 1
  - label: Style
    title: Color, typography and the digital candy store
    text: |-
      The color narrative stems directly from the logo's energy, offering a flexible palette that mirrors the rich variety of snacks. A deep, anchoring blue balances this out, serving as a visual anchor of trust and structure amid the vibrant hues. For typography, I chose Rubik for its soft, friendly curves that echo the brand's sweetness while maintaining pristine legibility across digital interfaces. Instagram acts as their primary digital candy store, so I established a strict set of visual guidelines to keep every piece of content cohesive, appetizing, and instantly recognizable.
    afterGroup: 3
  - label: Implementation
    title: Packaging, stickers and retail touchpoints
    text: |-
      Physical touchpoints require items that customers genuinely want to keep and display. I designed a series of lifestyle stickers engineered for phones, laptops, and notebooks, turning everyday users into organic brand advocates. These are complemented by custom packaging solutions and targeted promotional collateral. Every implemented detail is crafted to reinforce the brand experience and solidify Snacks Lovers' presence in the retail space.
