overviewText: |-
  See More Logistics needed a visual identity that matched their daily presence on Panamanian roads. The local transport sector often relies on dated, literal graphics that fail to communicate reliability to corporate buyers and public institutions. We built a functional brand system that positions the company as an accountable logistics partner for regional businesses and government contracts, shifting the perception from a basic transport provider to an institutional standard.
sections:
  - label: Challenges
    title: ''
    text: |-
      The primary obstacle was the lack of visual differentiation in the local freight market, where most operators rely on cluttered layouts that do not inspire corporate or governmental confidence. We had to solve for extreme physical scalability, ensuring the new identity maintained flawless legibility across small employee ID badges, customs paperwork, and 24 transport vehicles operating on national highways at high speeds.
    afterGroup: 1
  - label: Strategy
    title: Engineering structural grids for physical and screen applications
    text: |-
      We stripped away decorative clutter to focus entirely on functional legibility, developing a strict grid system using mathematical proportions in the brandbook to keep layouts consistent across every touchpoint. The logotype integrates a globe icon directly into the typography, anchoring the brand to Panama's role as a central Latin American transit hub. For the color palette, we paired deep industrial navy with electric blue to ensure high contrast in daylight and night operations. We applied this system systematically across heavy truck wraps, urban delivery vans, shipping manifests, tone of voice guidelines, and CR80 employee access badges equipped with QR verification.
    afterGroup: 4
  - label: Results
    title: A unified visual standard for a growing fleet
    text: |-
      By removing visual noise, we delivered an identity that functions as an operational tool rather than surface decoration. See More Logistics now operates with a consistent visual presence across its 24-unit fleet, internal documentation, and B2B sales materials. This unified standard reinforces their promise of transparency and gives them the structural leverage required to secure high-value contracts and drive regional business expansion.
