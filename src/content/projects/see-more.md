overviewText: SeeMore Logistics reached out to me needing a comprehensive brand
  refresh and their first-ever website. The company felt their existing visual
  identity was outdated, inconsistent, and misaligned with the quality of their
  operations. Furthermore, lacking an online presence made it difficult to
  establish trust with prospective partners. The primary objective was to
  modernize the brand and develop a highly professional digital platform,
  allowing the business to project a mature market presence and successfully
  compete for larger, higher-value clients.

    title: Vector refinement of the globe emblem and typographic update
    text: During the brand refresh, the primary requirement was preserving the
      signature globe emblem positioned between the words "Sea" and "More" in
      the logotype. The client prioritized keeping this symbol, as existing
      business partners recognized the brand through it. Work on the globe
      itself focused on refining vector geometry to ensure clarity across
      digital touchpoints and small sizes. However, the primary enhancement came
      through typography. Replacing the previous font, which felt slightly
      juvenile and failed to reflect the company's professional values, with a
      modern sans-serif typeface brought visual balance and a mature, reliable
      character to the identity.

  - label: Regional Identity, Motion & Fleet Branding
    title: Panama architectural motif, animation, and fleet livery design
    text: >-
      Because the logo update was intentionally conservative and focused
      primarily on typography and geometry, the standalone mark was not enough
      to fully convey the brand's core story, namely its strong Panamanian roots
      and strategic position in the local logistics hub. To complement the logo
      and firmly anchor the company in its region, I designed a custom visual
      motif. It features a detailed line-art illustration of the capital city
      skyline, highlighting iconic landmarks like recognizable skyscrapers and
      the Bridge of the Americas over the Panama Canal. Created as a flexible
      brand asset, it was developed for seamless application across various
      communication channels, from corporate merchandise and social media to the
      website, allowing SeeMore Logistics to communicate its regional identity
      in a clear, bold, and creative way.


      To reinforce this narrative and add visual energy, I transformed the
      static illustration into an animated sequence. This animated asset is
      heavily utilized across marketing and social media channels, where its
      subtle and refined motion catches the viewer's eye and drives engagement
      without being intrusive or overly aggressive. Furthermore, this core
      visual asset served as the foundation for the fleet branding concept.
      Applying the skyline motif to company trucks and delivery vehicles brings
      the brand identity directly onto the streets. As these vehicles navigate
      the city, they capture attention and build instant brand recall, operating
      as a highly creative, clean, and cost-effective mobile marketing channel.

  - label: Website Architecture & UX/UI Design
    title: UX/UI strategy, 3-second rule, and site architecture
    text: >-
      The primary challenge when designing the first website for SeeMore
      Logistics was establishing a digital presence from scratch that
      immediately positioned the company as a mature and reliable partner in the
      freight industry. Having no prior website, the project required a
      comprehensive analysis of user needs and a defined strategy to address
      core business requirements. The goal was to build a clean, straightforward
      content layout that cut through noise, clearly presenting services and
      enabling quick communication with prospective clients.


      When designing the Hero section, I relied on the 3-second rule: upon
      landing, a user must instantly understand where they are, what the company
      does, and what value it offers. This is precisely why the skyline
      animation was integrated using a lightweight Rive format. It serves as an
      immediate visual anchor that captures attention and contextualizes the
      brand within logistics and the region without impacting page performance.
      Beneath the headline, I placed two action buttons—one linking to services
      and the other to the contact form. This solution gives potential clients
      fast access to the exact information they are searching for, eliminating
      the need to scroll through the entire page.


      A key challenge specific to the Panamanian market involved the
      communication habits of local businesses. In Panama, business messaging
      heavily relies on WhatsApp, which clients use far more frequently than
      standard phone calls or web forms. To address this requirement, I
      prominently featured a direct WhatsApp button in the main navigation bar,
      allowing users to initiate instant communication at any point while
      browsing the site.


      Another challenge was instantly establishing credibility for a brand-new
      website. Directly below the Hero section, I integrated a client logo
      showcase to build social proof. This demonstrates that SeeMore Logistics
      is a trusted partner serving both major multinational corporations
      operating in Panama and local businesses. The remaining layout features
      clean, dedicated blocks for specific services, fleet capabilities, and
      company overview, closing with a focused contact module.

          regional sin ralentizar la carga del sitio. Debajo del encabezado,

          formulario de contacto). Esta solución brinda al cliente potencial un
          acceso rápido a la información que más busca, sin obligarlo a
          desplazarse por toda la página.
