overviewText: |-
  NRV Control Systems supplies advanced security and access control hardware to businesses across Panama. Their product range includes heavy duty document shredders, cash counters, airport scanners, biometric turnstiles with facial recognition, and automated parking barriers. Despite active social media marketing, the company struggled to generate meaningful business leads. They were missing out on corporate clients because their website was disorganized and their visual identity failed to project the authority needed in the B2B tech space.

  The primary objective of the project was to solve this lack of credibility and build trust with corporate buyers. I executed a complete brand transformation, which included a clean rebranding process followed by designing, developing, and testing a high converting website. The ultimate goal was to replace an outdated web setup with an efficient business tool that clearly reflects the advanced engineering behind their devices.
