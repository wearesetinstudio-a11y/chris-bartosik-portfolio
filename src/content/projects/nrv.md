overviewTitle: Moving Past Two Decades of Visual Stagnation
overviewText: |-
  For over twenty years, NRV Control Systems has supplied Panama’s commercial sector with access control hardware and security infrastructure. While their technical execution earned them market authority, their visual presence lagged behind their capability. I noticed that an outdated symbol, an inconsistent color palette, and a sharp disconnect between their social media and web platform were weakening client trust during high-stakes corporate procurement.
  
  My goal was to construct a sharp, system-driven visual framework built on clarity and order. Rather than copying standard industry tropes, I focused on a high-tech visual language that directly communicates reliability to facility managers and procurement directors.
sections:
  - label: Strategy
    title: Defining Core Pillars Through a Focused Sprint
    text: |-
      I initiated the rebranding process with a focused Brand Sprint alongside NRV’s leadership. Replacing agency overhead with direct collaboration, we mapped the operational requirements of technical directors and facility managers who evaluate critical infrastructure and demand clear information.
      
      Translating these strategic insights into visual direction, I established a minimalist, high-tech aesthetic. Setting these clear boundaries allowed me to build an agile design response focused on clarity and functional layout.
    afterGroup: 1
  - label: Mark
    title: Custom Typography Designed for Engineering Precision
    text: |-
      Security providers routinely rely on shield icons, padlocks, or network nodes. I bypassed these visual clichés to construct a distinctive typographic signet. Out of three proposed directions, the client selected a system built around custom letterforms.
      
      I crafted the mark using lowercase letterforms with strict geometric cuts and positioned the Control Systems descriptor in the upper right quadrant. This placement created subtle visual tension while clearly defining their enterprise service context.
    afterGroup: 2
  - label: System
    title: Technical Typography and Applied Spatial Concepts
    text: |-
      To restore visual order across brand touchpoints, I specified Archivo as the primary typeface. Its industrial proportions maintain high legibility across print proposals and digital views. I built the palette around a deep technical blue supported by neutral tones to establish clear hierarchy in documentation.
      
      Extending these rules into real-world environments, I developed concepts for physical touchpoints, including service vehicle fleet wraps, field staff apparel, signage, and corporate stationery. These spatial renders provided a clear preview of the brand presence in daily operations.
    afterGroup: 3
  - label: Delivery
    title: Pragmatic Workflow Handoff and Operational Tools
    text: |-
      I tailored my deliverables to match NRV’s internal workflow. I compiled a functional Brand Book covering core brand assets, usage rules, and typography, accompanied by vector and raster file exports.
      
      To support daily operations, I addressed their existing production habits. Because NRV creates everyday graphics in Canva, I provided native templates alongside primary Figma source files. I also delivered interactive motion assets built in Rive and conducted an onboarding walkthrough, giving their team full operational autonomy without risking visual integrity.

          Inicié el proceso de rediseño mediante un taller de Brand Sprint junto a la dirección de NRV. Prioricé la colaboración directa y las decisiones de negocio para analizar las necesidades de directores técnicos y responsables de compras que exigen información clara.

        title: Tipografía técnica y estructura funcional
        text: |-
