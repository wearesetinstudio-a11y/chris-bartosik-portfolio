overviewText: |-
  Bar Turysta operates as a foundational institution within the culinary landscape of Szczecin, serving generations of residents over decades of continuous operation. Approaching this conceptual rebranding as an independent designer, the objective was to untangle years of visual clutter and fragmented touchpoints that failed to reflect the true stature of the venue. The existing presentation relied on ad-hoc signage and uncoordinated messages that lacked cohesion and historical respect.

  The redesign framework focused on preserving the authentic mid-century industrial pedigree while translating it into a contemporary and legible design system. Rather than leaning into artificial nostalgia or westernized vintage tropes, the work extracted the raw, utilitarian honesty of Polish postwar graphic design and structured it for both physical architecture and digital channels.
sections:
  - label: Strategy
    title: Evolution over superficial reinvention
    text: |-
      Initial research centered on observing how Szczecin residents actually interact with the venue. In everyday language, locals never refer to a generic cafeteria; they simply say they are going to Turysta. This inherent naming carries immense emotional equity and brand recognition that requires no artificial manufacturing.

      The communication strategy pivots the visual hierarchy to place the word Turysta at the absolute center of the identity. Simultaneously, the framework bridges two distinct audiences. It honors the enduring habits of loyal patrons who remember the venue's origins while welcoming a younger demographic seeking authentic spaces anchored in real history rather than manufactured aesthetics.
    afterGroup: 1
  - label: Logotype
    title: A distinct voice for a local icon
    text: |-
      The new logotype emerged through the refinement of legacy letterforms, stripping away ornamental excess in favor of functional typography. The primary wordmark features a custom, fluid script that breaks the rigid geometry of the interior architecture while establishing a memorable brand signature. A supporting sans-serif descriptor provides necessary operational context without competing with the main headline.

      The expanded identity system includes versatile secondary marks tailored for diverse applications. These comprise the core logotype, a founding-year variant, a municipal-inspired seal acknowledging Szczecin roots, and a classic projecting oval sign designed for exterior mounting. This modular structure enables consistent scaling across miniature paper goods and large architectural markers alike.
    afterGroup: 2
  - label: Palette
    title: Tones grounded in physical matter
    text: |-
      Color selection derives directly from the venue's interior architecture, raw finishing materials, and the daily rhythm of kitchen operations. Eschewing abstract decorative palettes, the system relies on four foundational tones defined by their physical references. Night and Ink establish deep contrast and typographic legibility, while Snow and Mist introduce spatial balance and reference traditional tableware.

      Limiting the palette to absolute essentials enforced strict design discipline throughout the process. This constraint ensures that all visual communications remain instantly legible from a distance while retaining the austere, purposeful character native to historical Polish visual culture.
    afterGroup: 3
  - label: Facade
    title: The storefront as an active communication tool
    text: |-
      The exterior facade and display windows serve as the primary threshold between the venue and the street, dictating the decision to enter. Analysis of the existing structure revealed significant potential buried beneath layers of temporary window decals and disorganized announcements. The conceptual facade reorganizes this visual noise into a clear hierarchy of directional signage and precise operating hours inscribed on the glass.

      A dedicated glass panel acts as a dynamic surface for daily handwritten menus. Consequently, the storefront shifts from a passive backdrop into an active operational tool that directs pedestrian flow and signals culinary freshness before a guest ever steps inside.
    afterGroup: 4
  - label: Team
    title: The people behind the foundation
    text: |-
      Bar Turysta thrives on the individuals who serve residents and visitors every single day. Their direct engagement forms the core of the guest experience, making the staff the rightful center of the brand identity. The uniform design originated from observing the crew's daily routines and the practical physical demands of working across kitchen and dining spaces.

      The resulting apparel system balances professional durability with functional comfort for long service shifts. Upholding clean aprons and shirts featuring subtle branding unifies the interior space and reinforces operational professionalism in the eyes of every arriving guest.

  - label: Details
    title: Presence in daily micro-interactions
    text: |-
      Hospitality brands are defined by the tangible artifacts guests touch during their visit. The project established a comprehensive rollout across everyday objects, including bespoke takeout packaging, napkins, glassware, and durable tableware styled after traditional Polish dining halls.

      Each object functions as a narrative carrier, delivering an authentic sensory experience without resorting to caricature. For visitors, these items capture local character; for long-time residents, they trigger genuine familiarity anchored in trusted historical forms.

    title: Native nostalgia in digital spaces
    text: |-
      The final pillar translates the physical identity system into digital communication formats for social media. The core premise establishes that brand nostalgia requires no borrowed American tropes or generic aesthetic filters. Instead, the strategy utilizes authentic domestic design codes that resonate naturally with local audiences.

      Clean graphic layouts pair archival typography with direct, unpretentious editorial copy. This approach enables meaningful engagement with younger digital natives on their own terms, avoiding artificial fluff while maintaining deep respect for the culinary heritage of Bar Turysta.
